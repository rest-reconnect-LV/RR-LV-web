What is Rest & Reconnect?

Rest & Reconnect is a grassroots organization for disabled adults in Las Vegas. Anyone who identifies as having a disability or chronic condition is welcome. While not a medical support group, Rest & Reconnect LV focuses on disability advocacy and disability-forward events. Low-cost and low-hassle, you'll find no better place to rest and reconnect.

Who is this for?

Anyone living within Las Vegas who identifies as having a disability or the following:

● Chronic fatigue or pain
● Mental health conditions
● Neurodivergence
● Disability or fluctuating access needs

Fear not! At Rest & Reconnect LV, we encourage participants to share whatever they feel comfortable with. All that matters is that you're looking for an engaging and flexible space.

When and where?

Rest & Reconnect LV is rooted in accessibility and inclusivity. Every month, we aim to host a physically and mentally inclusive event within Las Vegas. Organizers also meet weekly to support Rest & Reconnect LV's mission.

Virtual events can be expected to be held on Zoom, with captions. For more on accessibility, view our Accessibility Page.

Rest & Reconnect has clear guidelines to keep our spaces respectful. We have a zero-tolerance policy for bigotry, harassment, or unsolicited medical advice.

We're growing! Rest & Reconnect LV is looking for co-organizers. Interested in exploring projects about disability and enriching our community? Check out the "Want to be an Organizer? for more information.
