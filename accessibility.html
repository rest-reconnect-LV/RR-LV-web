Accessibility

Making Space For Everyone

Rest & Reconnect LV is designed with accessibility in mind. I'm committed to making every event as welcoming and flexible as possible.

Virtual Meeting Accessibility

For online events, you can expect:

  • Live captions available during video calls
  • Text chat option - you can participate entirely through chat if you prefer
  • Camera optional - no pressure to turn your video on
  • Screen reader compatible platforms
  • No account required - just click a link to join
  • Browser-based - no special software downloads needed

If you need any specific accommodations for virtual meetings, please email me at rest-and-reconnect-social@proton.me and I'll do my best to arrange it.

In-Person Meeting Accessibility

For physical events, I will:

  • Choose wheelchair-accessible venues with automatic doors
  • Prioritize free or low-cost public spaces (libraries, community centers)
  • Provide location details in advance, including parking and public transit info
  • Keep events free to attend
  • Allow participants to bring their own food and drinks

If you have specific mobility, sensory, or other access needs for in-person events, let me know and I'll work to accommodate them.

General Flexibility

You're always welcome to:

  • Join late or leave early
  • Take breaks during meetings
  • Step away from conversation when you need to
  • Participate as much or as little as feels right for you

There's no attendance requirement and no expectation that you'll be "on" the whole time.

Questions Or Requests?

If you have accessibility questions or need a specific accommodation, email me at rest-and-reconnect-social@proton.me. I'll respond within a day or two.
