Guidelines

Community Expectations

● Respect the privacy of everyone attending.

● Do not pressure anyone to share personal information.

● Keep conversations supportive and constructive.

● Discrimination or harassment will not be tolerated.

How We Interact

● Allow space for people to participate at their own pace.

● Some participants may communicate slowly or need extra time.

● Silence, breaks, or stepping away from conversation are completely acceptable.

Accessibility Considerations

● Speak clearly and avoid talking over others.

● Be mindful that participants may be using screen readers or captions.

● Respect access needs and accommodations.

What This Space Is For

Rest & Reconnect is a grassroot organization focused on improving disability communities - not therapy, not medical treatment, not a sales pitch. We're here to connect, share interests, and rest without pressure.

We come here to rest, not perform.
