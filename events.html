Events

Every month, Rest & Reconnect hosts a virtual or in-person event. We now have an open call for Rest & Reconnect Organizers. Assisting with organizing keeps Rest & Reconnect sustainable, and all organizers learn transferable skills. From web design to project management, you're sure to learn something!

Wishing for an outdoor picnic planned with physically accommodating seating? Searching for neurodivergent-friendly events? Rest & Reconnect prepares each event with accessibility in mind. We believe that everyone deserves enriching experiences.

At events you can expect:

● Accessibility-first planning

● Explanation-free space

● Low-pressure socializing

● Fidget spinners welcome

We plan events based on interest and need. Details are shared on Eventbrite, Meetup, and Signal beforehand.
