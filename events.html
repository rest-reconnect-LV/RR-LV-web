Upcoming Event

What makes Las Vegas events so inaccessible? Whether disabled or not, join Rest & Reconnect LV for Accessibility Rant Hour: Las Vegas Events, a 45-minute virtual drop-in where locals can vent about access, cost, timing, and event culture. Feedback shared during this session will be logged and used to shape future accessibility-first events in Las Vegas.

What to Expect

  • Low-pressure social space
  • Work on something or just sit
  • Camera-free, explanation-free
  • Accessibility-first planning

Events may shift based on access needs and interest. Details are always shared ahead of time.

Future Event Interest

As part of ongoing planning and grant research, Rest & Reconnect LV is gathering interest in a free, disability-centered Tai Chi class in Las Vegas. Responses will be used only to assess interest and understand access needs for potential future events.
