Las Vegas Mutual Aid Organizations

This section is a reference list for community support organizations in Las Vegas. Inclusion here is not an endorsement. Please use personal judgment and verify details directly with each organization.

  • Arriba Las Vegas Worker Center — Grassroots org supporting immigrant worker-led movements for inclusion, dignity, and justice.
    https://www.arribalasvegas.org/
  • Fifth Sun Project — Indigenous justice, anti-colonial work, community empowerment; workshops, food preps, and relief efforts.
    https://linktr.ee/fifthsunproject
  • Free Self Defense LV — Self defense and awareness; recurring classes and workshops.
    https://linktr.ee/freeselfdefenselv
  • Las Vegas Liberation — Unhoused advocacy, food/resource sharing, coalition work; weekly food shares and distributions.
    https://lasvegasliberation.org/
    https://lasvegasliberation.org/about/
  • Project 150 — Support for homeless/displaced/disadvantaged high school students in Southern Nevada (food, clothing, hygiene, scholarships).
    https://www.project150.org/
  • The Obodo Collective — Food justice work, community partnerships; garden management and educational events.
    https://www.obodocollective.org/
  • The Solidarity Fridge — Indigenous-led food, land, and birth justice; community pantry/garden, seed shares, food preps.
    https://thesolidarityfridge.org/
