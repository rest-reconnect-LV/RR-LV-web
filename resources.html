Las Vegas Mutual Aid Organizations

Rest & Reconnect LV loves mutual aid. Here are some community support organizations in Las Vegas. Inclusion here is not an endorsement. Please use personal judgment and verify details directly with each organization.

◒ Arriba Las Vegas Worker Center — Grassroots organization supporting immigrant worker-led movements for dignity, inclusion, and labor rights.
https://www.arribalasvegas.org/

◒ Fifth Sun Project — Indigenous justice and anti-colonial organizing; workshops, food preparation efforts, and community relief projects.
https://linktr.ee/fifthsunproject

◒ Las Vegas Liberation — Unhoused advocacy, food and resource distribution, and community coalition work.
https://lasvegasliberation.org/
https://lasvegasliberation.org/about/

◒ Project 150 — Support for homeless and displaced high school students in Southern Nevada including food, clothing, hygiene products, and scholarships.
https://www.project150.org/

◒ The Obodo Collective — Community food justice initiatives, garden stewardship, and educational programming.
https://www.obodocollective.org/

◒ The Solidarity Fridge — Indigenous-led mutual aid focused on food access, land stewardship, and community pantry projects.
https://thesolidarityfridge.org/
