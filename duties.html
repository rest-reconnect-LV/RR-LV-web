Rest & Reconnect LV Organizer Roles

Rest & Reconnect LV loves Organizers!

Rest & Reconnect LV follows a sustainable organizer model; we prioritize collaboration, shared learning, and accessibility.

Organizers are not expected to arrive with professional experience. Instead, participants learn through hands-on work while building skills. Community organizing is complex, so Organizers may improve a broad range of skills.

All organizers should expect to attend regular weekly to bi-weekly meetings, when possible. We validate your experience! Organizers may request verification of volunteer or organizing hours. The goal of the Rest & Reconnect Organizing structure is simple: build community while helping disabled organizers gain life and practical experience.

Organizer Roles

Rest & Reconnect LV is currently recruiting volunteers for several organizing roles. Each role focuses on a different operational need of the project. Co-learning can be energizing! Organizers are encouraged to support one another and learn multiple skills.

Fund Guardian

The Fund Guardian organizes and tracks financial accounts and expenses for Rest & Reconnect LV. Responsibilities include maintaining a budget, tracking invoices, documenting reimbursements, and keeping records of expenses connected to events or supplies. Professional accounting experience is not required. However, anyone interested in becoming a Fund Guardian would benefit from enjoying working with numbers.

Admin Superhero

A jack-of-all-trades, the Admin Superhero role acts as a multi-faceted organizational position. Supporting the day-to-day coordination of Rest & Reconnect LV, the Admin Superhero is vital to a functioning team.

Responsibilities may include organizing shared cloud storage, maintaining files and notes, tracking communications, and helping manage internal documentation. As Rest & Reconnect LV prioritizes privacy and accessibility, the Admin Superhero may also explore open-source tools and emerging technological platforms. Love fast-paced learning? Being an Admin Superhero may be for you!

Event Builders

Rest & Reconnect LV is seeking two Event Planners.

Event Builders are integral to Rest & Reconnect LV's mission. Helping with design, coordination, and facilitating community gatherings, they are a recreational lifeline. Responsibilities include helping plan event logistics, assisting during meetups, supporting community activities, and helping guide small projects connected to events.

If you're interested in improving your communication and project management skills or are extrovert-leaning, consider the role of Rest & Reconnect LV's Event Builder.

What else should I expect as an Organizer?

Rest & Reconnect LV believes in the growth and development of its organizers.

Volunteers are encouraged to explore new responsibilities; learn the history of grassroots organizing, and participate in local non-disability issues. Mutual aid is increasingly Rest & Reconnect LV's outreach and sustainability plan. New ideas and initiatives to support disabled people in Las Vegas are always welcome.

At Rest & Reconnect LV, we practice leadership, strengthen confidence, and build sustainable community initiatives!
